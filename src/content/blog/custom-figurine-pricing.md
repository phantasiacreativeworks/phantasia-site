One of the most common questions we receive is, **"How much does a custom figurine cost?"**

The short answer is: it depends on the complexity of your project. Unlike mass-produced figures, every custom commission is sculpted from scratch, so pricing is based on the time and craftsmanship required.

## What affects the price?

### Character complexity

Simple characters with clean clothing are faster to sculpt than designs with detailed armor, flowing hair, accessories, or intricate patterns.

### Pose

Dynamic action poses, elaborate bases, or multiple characters require more planning and sculpting than a standard standing pose.

### Deliverables

Do you only need the digital STL, or would you also like a resin print? Physical production adds additional preparation, printing, and post-processing.

## Our pricing tiers

### Standard

Perfect for simple characters or busts. Includes a custom sculpt, turntable render, and print-ready STL.

### Premium

Our most popular option. Ideal for detailed characters with layered clothing, accessories, and higher levels of refinement.

### Deluxe

Designed for showcase pieces with complex compositions, display bases, or physical resin prints.

## How to get the most accurate quote

To provide an accurate estimate, simply send us:

* Character references
* Preferred pose
* Approximate size
* Whether you need a digital file or physical print

The more complete your references are, the more accurate your quote will be.

> Every project is unique, so we quote based on the work involved—not just the size of the figure. This ensures fair pricing and no unexpected surprises.
